Kuramoto synchronization

100 oscillators, each ticking at its own natural rhythm — no two exactly alike, drawn from a spread of frequencies. Each one nudges toward the current average phase of the whole group, weakly. No conductor, no oscillator aware of more than the group average. The question: at what coupling strength does the crowd stop ticking independently and snap into a shared rhythm? Theory gives an exact answer for this exact setup: K ≈ 2.0.
